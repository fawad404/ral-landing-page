WHAT IS THE COMPLIANCE PHASE?
----------------------------------------------------------------

RAL Connect is being built in two phases.

PHASE 1 — COMPLIANCE PHASE (What we are delivering today)
This is the phase you are currently watching.

The compliance phase is about helping Arizona assisted living
facility owners and managers run their facilities correctly,
stay on top of regulations, avoid violations, and manage their
team and documentation properly.

Think of it like the "operations backbone" of a facility.
It handles:
  - Who works there and what credentials they have
  - What tasks need to be done and by when
  - What incidents have happened and how they were handled
  - The facility profile and all its details
  - A daily stream of industry news and regulatory updates
    powered by AI (the Intelligence Hub)

This phase is designed so that a facility owner can log in
every day and immediately see: Is my facility compliant?
What is overdue? What do I need to act on today?

PHASE 2 — PLACEMENT PHASE (Coming next)
The placement phase is about connecting people. Specifically,
it is about connecting families looking for assisted living
placements with the right facilities, and connecting facilities
with vendors and service providers.

Think of it as the "marketplace" layer on top of the operations
backbone. It includes:
  - Availability tracking (how many beds are open)
  - Inquiry management (families asking about placement)
  - Matching engine (algorithmically matching families to facilities)
  - Support Network (facilities finding vendors and partners)
  - Social Boost (promoting facilities on social media)
  - Deal Room (facility acquisition and investment deals)

These features are fully built in the codebase but are hidden
from the navigation for now. They will be turned on when we
enter Phase 2.

In short:
  Compliance Phase = Run your facility correctly
  Placement Phase  = Grow your facility and connect with others

VIDEO 1 — INTRODUCTION TO RAL CONNECT
================================================================

Hello and welcome to RAL Connect.

RAL Connect is a purpose-built SaaS platform for Arizona
assisted living owners, managers, and vendors.

The platform has three types of users:

  1. ADMIN — The platform operator. Full control over everything.
  2. FACILITY — Assisted living facility owners and managers.
  3. VENDOR — Partners and service providers who support facilities.

Each user type has their own dashboard, their own set of tools,
and their own experience.

What you are seeing today is Phase 1 — the Compliance Phase.
This phase is focused entirely on helping facilities operate
correctly, stay compliant, and stay informed.

Let me walk you through each dashboard one by one.


================================================================
VIDEO 2 — ADMIN DASHBOARD WALKTHROUGH
================================================================

--- SECTION: Login ---

To log in, go to the RAL Connect login page.
Admins log in with their admin credentials.
The system uses secure JWT authentication with a 7-day session.

--- SECTION: Admin Dashboard Home ---

After logging in, the admin lands on the main dashboard.

At the top, you can see key platform statistics:
  - Total users registered on the platform
  - Total facilities in the system
  - Total vendors registered
  - Other summary metrics

This gives the admin an instant overview of platform activity.

--- SECTION: Users Management ---

Click on "Users" in the left sidebar.

Here the admin can see every user registered on the platform —
facilities, vendors, and other admins.

For each user, the admin can:
  - View their profile and account details
  - Approve a new registration
  - Activate or deactivate an account
  - See their role (Admin, Facility, or Vendor)

When a new facility or vendor registers, they are not
automatically active. The admin must approve them first.
This gives the admin full control over who is on the platform.

--- SECTION: Facilities Management ---

Click on "Facilities" in the sidebar.

This page shows every facility registered on the platform.

The admin can:
  - See all facility profiles
  - Approve a facility for full access
  - Flag a facility if there is a concern
  - View the facility's full profile including photos,
    care services, policies, and contact information

Facilities must be approved before they get full access.
This is part of the quality control process.

--- SECTION: Vendors Management ---

Click on "Vendors" in the sidebar.

This page shows all vendor accounts on the platform.

The admin can:
  - View all vendor profiles
  - Toggle visibility — control whether a vendor is
    visible to facilities or hidden from the platform
  - Edit vendor details and categories

Vendors are service providers who support assisted living
facilities — things like staffing agencies, training providers,
medical supply companies, and so on.

--- SECTION: Intelligence Hub ---

Click on "Intelligence Hub" in the sidebar.

This is one of the most powerful features of the platform.

The Intelligence Hub is an AI-powered content engine that
automatically monitors the Arizona assisted living market.

Every 2 hours, the system:
  1. Pulls in fresh articles and updates from 18 trusted sources
     including ADHS, CMS, McKnight's Senior Living,
     Senior Housing News, LeadingAge, AHCCCS, KFF, CDC,
     Arizona Legislature news, and local Phoenix news outlets.

  2. For each article, OpenAI automatically generates:
     - A plain-English headline written for facility owners
     - A short 2-3 sentence summary
     - A "What This Means" section explaining the real impact
       on Arizona assisted living owners
     - An Operator Takeaway with one concrete action
     - A Facebook post draft ready to copy and post
     - An Email Blurb for newsletter use
     - A Relevance Score from 1 to 10

  3. Everything is stored and organized for admin review.

On the main Intelligence Hub page, the admin can see all
imported articles with these filters:
  - Search by keyword
  - Filter by Category (Arizona Regulations, Compliance,
    ALTCS/Medicaid, Staffing, Memory Care, and more)
  - Filter by Source (which publication it came from)
  - Filter by Status (Pending, Processing, Processed,
    Approved, Rejected)
  - Filter by Priority (Critical, High, Normal)
  - Filter by Approved status
  - Filter by Ready to Post status
  - Filter by Date Range

--- SECTION: Intelligence Hub — Article Detail ---

Click on any article to open the detail view.

Here you can see the full AI output:
  - The original article title and source
  - The AI-generated headline
  - The summary
  - What This Means
  - The Operator Takeaway
  - The Facebook Post — with a Copy button to instantly
    copy the text to your clipboard for posting
  - The Email Blurb

All of these fields are fully editable. If you want to
adjust the tone or add a personal touch, just click and edit.

From this page you can also:
  - Approve or reject the article
  - Mark it as Ready to Post
  - Set the priority level
  - Add internal notes
  - Re-run the AI to generate a fresh version of the output

--- SECTION: Intelligence Hub — Sources ---

Click on "Sources" within the Intelligence Hub section.

Here you can see all 18 RSS sources the system monitors.
Sources are organized into three tiers:
  - Tier 1: Core sources (ADHS, CMS, McKnight's, etc.)
  - Tier 2: High value sources (AHCCCS, KFF, CDC, etc.)
  - Tier 3: Local news (AZ Central, Phoenix Business Journal, etc.)

The admin can:
  - Add a new source
  - Edit an existing source
  - Disable a source without deleting it
  - Toggle sources active or inactive

--- SECTION: CMS Content ---

Click on "CMS Content" in the sidebar.

This is where the admin manages content that appears on the
platform — announcements, pages, and other static content.

--- SECTION: Reports ---

Click on "Reports" in the sidebar.

The reports section shows platform analytics and activity
summaries — user growth, facility activity, compliance
statistics, and other metrics.

--- SECTION: Configuration ---

Click on "Configuration" in the sidebar.

Here the admin controls platform-wide settings such as
category limits, platform rules, and system configuration.

VIDEO 3 — FACILITY DASHBOARD WALKTHROUGH
================================================================

--- SECTION: Login as Facility ---

Facility owners and managers log in with their facility account.
After login, they land on their facility dashboard.

--- SECTION: Facility Dashboard Home ---

The facility home page gives the owner or manager an instant
compliance overview of their facility.

At the top, you can see compliance stats:
  - Total tasks and how many are overdue
  - Total incidents and their status
  - Staff credentials and how many are expiring soon
  - Overall compliance health

Below the stats, there is an activity log showing recent
actions and a compliance alerts panel that surfaces the
most urgent items — overdue tasks, expiring credentials,
and open incidents.

This page is designed so that when a manager opens their
laptop in the morning, they can see in 10 seconds exactly
what needs attention today.

--- SECTION: Facility Profile ---

Click on "Facility Profile" in the sidebar.

This is where the facility manages their full profile.

The profile is organized into tabs:

  Overview Tab:
    - Facility name, description, and location
    - Bed capacity and care level
    - License number and type

  Care Services Tab:
    - What types of care the facility provides
    - Specialties like memory care

  Photos Tab:
    - Upload facility photos
    - Photos are stored securely via Cloudinary
    - Delete photos you no longer want

  Policies Tab:
    - House rules and policies
    - Visitor policies
    - Other facility-specific guidelines

  Contact Tab:
    - Address, phone, email
    - Primary contact person

Changes to the profile are saved immediately. The admin
can see and review the profile from the admin panel.

--- SECTION: Compliance Center ---

Click on "Compliance" in the sidebar.

This is the most important section for the compliance phase.
The Compliance Center has three tabs: Tasks, Incidents,
and Staff Credentials.

-- TASKS TAB --

Tasks are the day-to-day compliance items a facility must
complete. Examples include medication reviews, safety
inspections, training completions, and documentation updates.

On this tab, the manager can:
  - See all tasks with their due dates and status
  - Create a new task with a title, description,
    assigned staff member, due date, and category
  - Mark a task as complete
  - Edit a task
  - Delete a task

The system automatically marks tasks as OVERDUE when their
due date has passed without being completed. Overdue tasks
show up as alerts on the dashboard home page.

Tasks have statuses:
  - Pending — not started
  - In Progress — being worked on
  - Completed — done
  - Overdue — past the due date

-- INCIDENTS TAB --

Incidents are events that happen at the facility that must
be documented. Arizona regulations require facilities to
track and report certain types of incidents.

On this tab, the manager can:
  - Log a new incident with a title, description, date,
    severity level, and the resident or staff member involved
  - Update the status of an incident as it is investigated
  - Mark an incident as resolved
  - View the full incident history

Incident severity levels:
  - Low — minor issue, no immediate risk
  - Medium — requires follow-up
  - High — serious incident requiring documentation
  - Critical — immediate action required

-- STAFF CREDENTIALS TAB --

Staff credentials track licenses, certifications, and
training for every staff member at the facility.

In Arizona, assisted living staff must maintain current
licenses and certifications. Tracking expiration dates
manually is error-prone and risky.

This tab automates that.

The manager can:
  - Add a credential record for any staff member
    (name, credential type, license number,
    issue date, expiration date)
  - See all credentials at a glance
  - Instantly see which ones are expiring soon or expired

The system automatically:
  - Flags credentials as EXPIRING SOON when they are
    within 30 days of expiration
  - Marks credentials as EXPIRED when the date has passed
  - Surfaces these as alerts on the dashboard home page

This means the manager never has to manually check
expiration dates. The system does it automatically.

Credential statuses:
  - Active — current and valid
  - Expiring Soon — expires within 30 days
  - Expired — past the expiration date

VIDEO 5 — PHASE 2 PREVIEW (What is Coming Next)
================================================================

Now that you have seen Phase 1 — the Compliance Phase —
let me briefly explain what is coming in Phase 2.

Phase 2 is the Placement Phase. This is when RAL Connect
becomes a full marketplace and placement engine.

Here is what will be unlocked:

  AVAILABILITY TRACKING
  Facilities will be able to update their available beds
  in real time. Families and placement agents searching
  for a facility can see which ones have openings.

  INQUIRY MANAGEMENT
  When a family reaches out about placement, the inquiry
  is automatically captured in the system. Facilities
  receive and respond to inquiries directly in the platform.

  MATCHING ENGINE
  The platform algorithmically matches families to the
  right facilities based on care needs, location, budget,
  and bed availability.

  SUPPORT NETWORK
  Facilities can browse a directory of trusted vendors
  and service providers — staffing agencies, training
  companies, medical suppliers, and more.

  SOCIAL BOOST
  Facilities can submit their story, photos, and updates
  for the RAL Connect team to boost on social media.
  This drives visibility and fills beds faster.

  DEAL ROOM
  A secure space for facility acquisition discussions,
  investment opportunities, and business deals between
  owners, investors, and brokers.

All of these features are fully built in the backend
right now. They are simply hidden from the navigation
until we enter Phase 2 and turn them on.

This means Phase 2 deployment will be fast — it is not
a full rebuild. It is an activation of what is already there.
